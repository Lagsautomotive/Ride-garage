Backup & restore

Your garage lives on this device by default. Cloud Garage Sync is optional and never runs automatically. Back up your local records before clearing your browser or moving to another device. Restoring from a file replaces local records after confirmation. Export your current garage first and check that the backup downloaded successfully. Photos make the backup large. Social accounts/posts are not included.
