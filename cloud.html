How syncing works

Cloud copies are made only when you tap Save. Merging adds missing records to the current device and keeps differing versions. No automatic uploading, deleting or overwriting occurs.

Cloud copies can include GPS routes, saved places and motorcycle photos. A copy has an approximately 7.8 MB upload limit. Download a local backup before merging on another device.
