Privacy Policy

Updated October 14, 2026

Ride Garage helps riders track motorcycle builds, trips, places, maintenance and fuel, and offers an optional community.

Information processed

Garage entries, saved locations, notes and photos are stored on your device in browser/app storage unless you choose to share a community post. You can export your local records using Backup & restore.

If you create a community account, we process your email address, authentication information, rider username, optional bike description, posts, comments, likes, reports, blocks and uploaded images. We also process technical information required to operate and secure the service.

Optional beta tester program

If you choose to join the beta tester program while signed in to Social, we record your account ID, enrollment date, last check-in time and a count of check-ins when you visit the Social tab. Check-ins are limited to once every 30 minutes. We use this information only to understand beta participation and which improvements to prioritize. Visitors who do not enroll are not included in these statistics. Your local garage, ride routes, fuel records and private photos are not uploaded by this feature. You can leave the beta program from the Social tab without deleting your community account. Deleting your community account also deletes the associated beta enrollment record.

How information is used

We use account data to authenticate riders, display community content, enforce community rules, investigate reports and provide app functionality. Posts and profile usernames may be visible to other users. Avoid posting sensitive personal information.

Service providers

Community authentication, database and image storage are provided using Supabase. The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. These services may process information needed to provide their services.

Data sharing and retention

We do not offer riders' personal data for sale. Community content is shared with other riders as part of the features you choose to use. Data can remain while your account is active; deletion requests are reviewed and processed, subject to legitimate security or legal retention needs. No fixed retention schedule is currently promised.

Your choices

You can remove local records in the app or use backup/restore to manage your copy. You can delete your own community posts, block others and request deletion of your community account and associated data by contacting us from the email on your account at ridegarageapp@gmail.com. We may verify ownership before processing a request. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete a community account.

Security and updates

We use authentication and access controls for the community, but no online system can guarantee absolute security. We may update this notice as features change.
